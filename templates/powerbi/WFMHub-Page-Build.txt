1 DAILY WFM COMMAND
Cards: LOBs on target, demand vs forecast, confirmed no show, current
productive capacity gap. Charts: service level by management LOB and required /
observed / productive 15-minute capacity. Bottom: ordered WFM findings.

2 SERVICE & SL DRIVERS
Cards: SL, demand variance, capacity loss and primary supported pressure signal.
Charts: 15-minute SL/demand and five pressure signals. Bottom: exact interval /
queue evidence. Signals are review leads, not causal SL-point attribution.

3 STAFF PREPARATION
Cards: peak required FTE, net scheduled FTE, peak shortage and critical
intervals. Charts: required vs scheduled and LOB/day coverage. PTO/Away is
deducted. Missing required FTE stays blank. No language eligibility is guessed.

4 WORKFORCE REALISATION
Cards: observed/elapsed, productive/observed, productive/elapsed and unexplained
hours. Charts: governed status composition and auditable capacity bridge.

5 SCHEDULE INTEGRITY
Cards: displaced shifts/hours, recurring agents and early leaves. Charts:
published vs observed placement and supported patterns. Agent Status first,
LILO fallback, completed days only; PTO/Away excluded. Not adherence.

6 FORECAST ACCURACY
Cards: 15-minute WAPE accuracy, bias, weighted AHT error and top-demand-decile
accuracy. Charts: actual vs forecast and LOB/weekday accuracy. Bottom: misses.

7 DATA READINESS
Cards: fresh sources, inbound-entry queue mapping, observed-minute status
mapping and blocking issues. Charts: freshness and governed update pipeline.
