WFMHUB PREMIUM PAGE BUILD
=========================

GLOBAL
Use 16:9. Navy header #0B1F33, teal #007C83, gold #D6A84B, canvas #F4F7F9.
Keep Date, LOB and Team slicers in the same upper band and sync them across
pages. Each page uses one decision question and no more than six headline KPIs.

1 EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Cards: Service Level, Routing Availability, Staffing Gap, No Show HC,
PCS Average, Final Absence. Trend: SL and volume by date. Matrix: LOB pulse.

2 SERVICE & FORECAST
Cards: Offered, Answered, SL, AHT, Volume Variance. Line/column: hourly forecast
volume vs actual offered; forecast-only drill retains native 15-minute rows.
Matrix: Service Scope > Queue by hour.

3 ATTENDANCE CONTROL
Cards: Scheduled HC, Present HC, No Show HC, Callout HC, Late Minutes,
Early Leave Minutes. Table: exact agent callout list. Timeline/detail:
FactAttendanceGap with exact gap start/end.

4 PCS PERFORMANCE & COACHING
Cards: PCS Average, Participation, Valid PCS, Low Scores. Bar: LOB then agent
PCS. Trend: daily PCS. Detail: FactPCSCoaching with Call ID and comment.

5 STAFFING & CAPACITY
Cards: Scheduled FTE, Observed FTE, Gap FTE. Combo chart by 15-minute interval.
Matrix by LOB/language. Forecast FTE remains blank when source has volume only.

6 ABSENCE & SHRINKAGE
Cards: Final Absence %, Final Shrinkage %, Absence Minutes, Review HC. Stacked
bar by Activities category. Detail lists empty shifts, unmapped activities and
uncorrected/partial cases using Final Ledger Status.

7 DATA QUALITY & GOVERNANCE
Cards: latest data date, quality issues, critical issues. Source status table.
Issue table. Finding table. Show manifest/schema/rule versions in an info panel.
